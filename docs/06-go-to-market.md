# Go-to-market, monetization and community

## 1. Positioning

Category: AI Minecraft architectural blueprint generator. Promise: "From idea to an installable, editable build in under a minute." Differentiate on reliable geometry, transparent limits, version-correct exports and remixable community blueprints—not on "AI" alone.

Initial niche: English-speaking Java Edition creators building small architecture for maps, servers and videos. Do not market to all Minecraft players at once.

## 2. Launch wedge

Start with three highly visual categories: survival houses, fantasy towers and bridges. Each receives curated styles, palettes, example prompts and SEO landing pages. Expand only when success/download data proves quality.

Acquisition loop:

```text
SEO/short video -> interactive example -> generation -> preview/download
       ^                                      |
       +---- public page <- publish/remix ----+
```

Every approved build produces a canonical page, preview image, material list, dimensions, safe prompt summary, remix button and version badge. Index useful pages; noindex thin/duplicate/private/pending pages.

## 3. Channels

- YouTube Shorts/TikTok: prompt, rotating preview, in-game result in 20 seconds.
- YouTube creators: free credits and tracked links; pay only after revenue exists.
- Reddit/Discord: show builds and ask for critique, never spam links.
- SEO clusters: "Minecraft [style] house generator", "[object] mcfunction", build dimensions and material guides.
- Creator partnerships: server hosts, map makers, educators and command/tutorial channels.
- Product loop: share card and optional tasteful credit in metadata, never forced branding inside the build.

## 4. Community plan

- Weekly themed challenge; winners featured, not paid initially.
- Remix lineage and creator attribution.
- Collections such as "Starter Village" assembled from independent builds.
- Discord channels for prompts, showcase, failed builds and feature voting.
- Public quality rubric and transparent moderation appeals.
- Recognize creators for downloads/remixes, not raw upload volume.

Manual moderation is limited to featured/public submissions. Private generations rely on automated safety plus reports if later published.

## 5. Pricing experiments

Do not launch with unlimited use. Suggested hypotheses, not permanent prices:

- Free: one generation/day, public gallery/downloads, private by default.
- Credit pack A: 10 generations for EUR 2.99.
- Creator A: EUR 7.99/month for 50 credits, private projects and no ads.
- Pro later: EUR 14.99/month for 150 credits, larger limits, additional formats and priority.

Run one variable at a time. Show quota only after users experience a successful build. Price tests require minimum sample sizes and must measure contribution margin, not only checkout rate.

Revenue extensions after product-market evidence: `.schem`/Litematica exports, step-by-step build mode, team libraries, API, education licenses, server-host integrations and a curated premium blueprint marketplace.

## 6. Metrics and decision gates

North-star: weekly users who successfully preview and download at least one structure.

Funnel: landing -> demo -> prompt -> signup -> successful generation -> preview interaction -> download -> second-week return -> paid.

Guardrails: successful-generation cost, failure/refund rate, moderation rate, support tickets, report rate and p95 latency.

Decision gates:

- Do not add a second game edition until 30% preview-to-download and 15% D7 retention.
- Do not buy ads until organic free-to-paid conversion and contribution margin are positive.
- Do not add huge builds until the 5,000-block experience is reliable.
- Pause free growth if abuse or generation cost cannot be bounded.
- Pivot toward tutorials/material lists if users preview builds but rarely download.

## 7. First 30 content assets

Create ten original golden builds across the three launch categories, each with an in-game screenshot, rotating preview and prompt lesson. Turn each into one gallery page, one short video and one educational post. This yields 30 assets without fabricating community activity.

## 8. Legal/brand boundaries

- Use a distinct name, visual identity, copy and implementation.
- Never copy competitor prompts, structures, screenshots or community data.
- State clearly that the product is unofficial and not affiliated with Mojang/Microsoft.
- Reject or restrict public/commercial claims around protected characters and brands.
- Use original or properly licensed textures and marketing assets.
